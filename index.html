Why hosts use it

Built for the person actually running the room.

Not a survey tool repurposed for events. Tally is shaped around the moment you're standing in front of people and need the room to move with you.

01

Zero friction to join

A phone and a browser is all it takes. No app store, no sign-up, no dead ends between a QR scan and a vote.

02

A dashboard built for you

A live preview shows exactly what your audience sees, next to your controls — and keyboard shortcuts keep the room moving without breaking eye contact with your laptop.

03

Polls and slides, one deck

Mix voting questions with full-screen images, audio, or announcement slides — a complete session flow, not just a string of multiple-choice questions.

The actual sequence

From empty deck to exported report, in four steps.

01

Build

Assemble your deck: polls, slides, or both, in any order. Generate a batch of questions on a topic, or write them by hand.

02

Start

Starting a session gives you a join code and QR. Attendees scan or type it on their own phone — nothing to install.

03

Run

Open one item at a time. Votes tally live on your dashboard, with a preview of exactly what attendees see.

04

End & export

Ending a session generates a report and exports your data. Import it later to reuse questions or compare results.

Ready when you are

Your next session starts whenever you open the tab.

No account to create, nothing to install first. Build a deck and you're running a live room in minutes.
